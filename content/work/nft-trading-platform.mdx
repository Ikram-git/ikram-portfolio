## Context

A group web application for storing, buying and selling NFTs, built as a
client/API split — a React front end talking to a Node.js backend with MongoDB
persistence. My focus was the trading flows and keeping them secure end to
end.

## What I built

- **Marketplace flows** — listing, browsing, and buying NFTs, with ownership
  recorded against user accounts in MongoDB.
- **Encrypted trading** — RSA encryption protecting the transaction exchange,
  so trades can't be tampered with or replayed in transit.
- **Client/API architecture** — a clean separation between the React client
  and the Node API, which let the group work in parallel without stepping on
  each other.

## Why it matters

An early pass at the same questions my later production work keeps asking:
who owns what, how do you prove it, and how do you make the transfer
tamper-proof — the difference is that here we had to answer them with our own
encryption layer rather than a chain's.
