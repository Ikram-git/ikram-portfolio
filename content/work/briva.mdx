## Context

Briva ([meetbriva.com](https://meetbriva.com)) is a production SaaS I built and
run solo. It records or ingests meeting audio, transcribes it — including
multilingual, code-mixed Hong Kong conversations — and produces AI summaries,
key decisions and action items, with export to PDF/email and Google Calendar
integration.

## Constraint

Speech-to-text is the product's foundation and its biggest risk. No single
engine is best across accents, price and reliability — and Hong Kong meetings are
often code-mixed (English and Cantonese in the same sentence), which trips up
engines tuned for one language. Depending on a single provider means their
outages, pricing changes and language gaps become mine. As a solo build, all of
that has to work without a team to lean on.

## What I built

- An **STT adapter pattern** across Deepgram and Google Cloud STT behind one
  interface, so the transcription provider is swappable.
- Summarisation and action-item extraction with Claude.
- The full stack: Next.js, Express, TypeScript, Supabase and Tailwind, plus
  calendar integration and PDF/email export.

## Decisions and trade-offs

**An adapter over locking to one STT provider.** Putting Deepgram and Google
Cloud STT behind a single interface costs an abstraction layer and a normalised
transcript format up front, but it means I can route by language or cost, and
swap or fall back when a provider underperforms on code-mixed audio — without
touching the rest of the app. Committing to one engine would have been simpler to
build and far more fragile to run.

**Supabase over rolling my own backend.** Leaning on Supabase for auth, database
and storage traded some control for the ability to ship and iterate solo — the
right call for a one-person product where time is the scarcest resource.

## Outcome

A live product at [meetbriva.com](https://meetbriva.com): it records or ingests a
meeting, transcribes code-mixed audio, and returns summaries, decisions and
action items with calendar and export integration — built and operated end to end
by one person.
